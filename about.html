Who we are

An engineering business, not a reseller

Gridora designs, supplies and commissions battery energy storage systems for industry, infrastructure and renewable generation.

We work at both ends of the range: a single outdoor cabinet behind a factory switchboard, and multi-block yards connected at transmission voltage. The engineering discipline is the same in both cases — understand the load, size the system against it, and prove the result at commissioning rather than on a datasheet.

Our teams cover electrical design, controls, integration and commissioning, and stay with a project through handover and the first year of operation.

What we stand for

Mission · Vision · Values

The commitments behind every system we ship

01

Our Mission

To make stored energy an ordinary part of how industry and infrastructure are powered — engineered properly, priced honestly and supported for the life of the asset.

02

Our Vision

Networks that run on generation when it is available and on storage when it is not, without the site or the operator having to think about which.

03

Our Values

Say what the system will do and then demonstrate it. Design for the person who has to service it at two in the morning. Never trade a safety layer for a commercial one.

04

Our Commitment

A named engineer from enquiry through to handover, documentation that matches what was actually installed, and spares and support planned before commissioning rather than after.

Our commitment

Four things we hold ourselves to

  • Engineering Excellence

    Systems are designed from the load profile up, not selected from a catalogue and made to fit.

  • Sustainable Innovation

    Longer service life and higher round-trip efficiency do more for the carbon case than any claim on a brochure.

  • Global Quality Standards

    Cells, conversion and enclosures are specified against recognised international standards and tested as an assembly.

  • Customer-Centric Approach

    The people who scoped the project stay on it through commissioning, handover and the first year of operation.
