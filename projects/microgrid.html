About this project

Overview

A campus with its own distribution network, rooftop and ground-mount solar, and a standby generator that had been running far more often than anyone wanted. The brief was to island cleanly and to cut generator hours rather than to chase a payback on the tariff.

Challenge

Islanding is the easy half. The difficult parts are holding voltage and frequency once islanded with a load that changes quickly, sequencing solar and generator against each other without hunting, and resynchronising to the incoming supply without the campus noticing.

Solution

Grid-forming conversion establishes the island reference, so the solar inverters have something to follow when the grid is absent. A single controller sequences the sources: solar first, storage to fill, generator only when the state of charge falls below a set floor and the forecast does not recover it. Resynchronisation is closed-transition, checked at commissioning with a witnessed test.

Configuration

  • Grid-forming power conversion with black-start capability
  • Microgrid controller sequencing solar, storage and generator
  • Closed-transition islanding and resynchronisation
  • Witnessed islanding test as part of handover
  • Load shedding scheme for extended island operation
