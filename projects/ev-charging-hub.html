About this project

Overview

A highway charging site where the available distribution connection was well below the sum of the charger ratings. Upgrading the connection was quoted with a long lead time, which made the site unviable on that route.

Challenge

Charging demand is spiky and correlated: vehicles arrive together, charge hard for twenty minutes and leave. Sizing the connection for the worst-case simultaneous draw means paying for capacity that is idle almost all the time, and the demand charge on a site like this can exceed the energy charge.

Solution

Storage sits between the connection and the charger bus and supplies the difference during bursts, recharging in the gaps. The connection is sized to the average draw across the day rather than the peak, and the demand charge becomes predictable because the controller enforces a hard import limit. Adding charge points later is a controller setting rather than another connection application.

Configuration

  • Storage between the utility connection and the charger bus
  • Hard import limit enforced by the site controller
  • Recharge scheduled into low-occupancy windows
  • Headroom reserved for additional charge points
  • Session-level metering retained for the operator
