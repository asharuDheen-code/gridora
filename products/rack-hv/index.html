Subsystems

Four independent layers

What sits behind the specification

Safety

Detection, isolation and suppression operate as independent layers. Gas and smoke detection, rack-level disconnection and a suppression interface are separately powered and separately monitored, so no single failure removes more than one layer.

Cooling

A closed-loop liquid circuit holds the pack inside its design temperature band across the ambient range. Cell-to-cell temperature spread is monitored continuously — the spread matters more for ageing than the absolute temperature does.

Monitoring

Cell voltages, currents, temperatures, insulation resistance and state of health are logged locally and pushed to whatever supervisory system the site already runs. Alarms carry a cause, not just a code.

Protection

Overcurrent, overvoltage, undervoltage, reverse polarity, insulation fault and thermal runaway precursors each have a defined response. The response is documented, tested at commissioning and repeatable on demand.
