Sizing starts with the load, not the cabinet

Two numbers set the system: how much power it has to deliver at once, and for how long. Power decides the conversion stage; energy decides how many racks sit behind it. A site with a short, sharp peak needs a different balance from one that has to run islanded overnight.

Send a year of interval data if you have it. If you do not, a month is usually enough to see the shape.
