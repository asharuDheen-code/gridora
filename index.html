Overview

01 / 05

Powering Progress with Clean Energy

Our battery energy storage systems help organisations store energy, reduce costs and build a more resilient energy future.

Most sites do not have an energy problem so much as a timing problem. Generation peaks at midday, demand peaks in the evening, and the tariff punishes the gap between them. Storage closes that gap.

We design, supply and commission systems from a single 215 kWh cabinet behind a factory switchboard up to multi-block yards connected at transmission voltage — the same controls, the same safety philosophy, sized to the job.

Engineered for Performance

A storage system is six subsystems that have to agree with each other. We build them as one product so that the failure of any single layer is contained by the next.
